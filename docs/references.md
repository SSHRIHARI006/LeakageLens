# References used by LeakageLens documentation and design

The archive contained 20 files but 18 distinct works because Briere et al. and Rosenblatt et al. each appear as a preprint and a later/published version. References below list the version used where identifiable. The breast-cancer comparison and Dong sensor paper were reviewed but are not used as design authority.

- Apicella, A., Isgro, F., & Prevete, R. (2025). *Don't push the button! Exploring data leakage risks in machine learning and transfer learning*. Artificial Intelligence Review, 58, 339. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-025-11326-3
- Bernett, J., Blumenthal, D. B., Grimm, D. G., et al. (2024). *Guiding questions to avoid data leakage in biological machine learning applications*. Nature Methods, 21, 1444-1453. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02362-y
- Briere, G., Stosskopf, T., Loire, B., & Baudot, A. (2025). *Benchmarking the impact of data leakage on the performance of knowledge graph embedding models for biomedical link prediction*. Archive supplies preprint and published-version files.
- Disoki, M. B., & Sonbol, R. (year not stated in supplied PDF). *Inflated Performance in fMRI-Based ASD Diagnosis: A Reproducibility Study of Data Leakage in Feature Selection Pipelines*.
- Draoui, T., ElMabrouk, Z., Mkaouer, M. W., et al. (2026). *Understanding how data leakage manifests in real-world machine learning notebooks*. Information and Software Technology, 200, 108336. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infsof.2026.108336
